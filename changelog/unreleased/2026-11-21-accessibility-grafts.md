- `El::accessibility_graft` hosts a foreign AccessKit tree under an element, as AccessKit grafts one: `Tx::update_accessibility_subtree` pushes its nodes (bounds from the element's top-left), Rune keeps the latest so a reader that attaches later gets it whole, sends it only once its graft node is out, and routes actions on its nodes to `App::on_accessibility_action`; `Tx::remove_accessibility_subtree` forgets it.
