- **A page's accessibility tree no longer crashes AccessKit when the page has a child of its own.** A grafted element such as a web page's surface could have Rune children, like the IME caret for an editable field or a hover overlay. Rune emitted those children as nodes but left them out of the graft's child list, so AccessKit saw nodes with no parent in the update and panicked. A graft node's children now stay out of the tree.
